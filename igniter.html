Pyrotechnic Igniter System

Igniter system development focused on reliable ignition performance, robust mechanical retention, low-cost manufacturability, and repeatable validation testing.

Machined Igniter Bracket Assembly

Prototype igniter bracket assembly manufactured using a manual lathe and drill press with an emphasis on low-cost fabrication and rapid iteration.

The assembly provided reliable igniter retention while remaining lightweight, simple to machine, and easy to integrate into the propulsion system.

Bracket Concept Development

Following prototype validation, the igniter retention concept evolved into a modular bracket system optimized for alignment, repeatability, and installation consistency.

The redesign reduced unnecessary complexity while improving structural robustness under dynamic loading conditions while maintaining low manufacturing cost.

Ignition Testing & Validation

The igniter utilized an APCP-based thermite pyrotechnic mixture initiated through a resistive bridgewire ignition system.

When electrical current is applied, the bridgewire rapidly heats and initiates combustion of the energetic compound, producing reliable ignition energy for motor startup.

Testing validated ignition reliability, combustion consistency, flame strength, and overall system robustness prior to propulsion system integration.
