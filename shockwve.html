SHOCKWVE

ShockWve is a custom propulsion software suite that standardizes rocket engine analysis, nozzle contouring, and simulation-ready export. It bridges analytical propulsion modeling with CFD validation workflows.

NASA CEA-ready workflow: enter chamber temperature, gamma, gas constant, pressure, thrust, and expansion ratio outputs from NASA CEA to move directly from combustion analysis into nozzle contour generation.
